New paper on the arXiv: [BayeSN × Dovekie: Joint Photometric Cross-calibration and SED Modelling of Type Ia Supernovae](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19429). We jointly train the BayeSN model and constrain photometric cross-calibration between instruments, using calibration constraints from Dovekie as a prior.
