New paper on the arXiv: [BayeSN-TD: Time Delay and H₀ Estimation for Lensed SN H0pe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11719). We measure time delays and magnifications for the gravitationally lensed supernova H0pe, yielding $H_0=69.3^{+12.6}_{-7.8}$ km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹.
