Software

jax-SNANA

GPU-accelerated supernova simulation, light curve fitting and cosmology

I developed jax-SNANA, a JAX implementation of the SNANA supernova analysis framework. It supports survey simulations, light curve fitting, distance estimation and cosmological inference on CPUs and GPUs, with a compiled simulator for simulation-based inference.

GitHub Documentation Paper

BayeSN

Next-generation SED model for type Ia supernovae

I lead development of BayeSN, a hierarchical Bayesian spectral energy distribution model that infers the intrinsic properties of SNe Ia jointly with extinction caused by dust. Using my GPU-accelerated BayeSN framework, I have demonstrated intrinsic differences between SNe Ia in different environments, with significant implications for cosmology.

BayeSN is integrated into the SNANA supernova cosmology pipeline.

Other groups have used BayeSN to analyse supernovae observed with JWST, including SN 2023adsy (Vinkó & Regős 2025) and SN 2023aeax (Pierel et al. 2025).

Python JAX NumPyro GPU

BayeSN-TD

Time-delay inference for gravitationally lensed Type Ia supernovae

I developed BayeSN-TD, an adaptation of BayeSN for strongly lensed supernovae, and applied it to measure the local expansion rate of the universe using the lensed SN H0pe.

The accompanying paper is published in MNRAS.

Python JAX NumPyro
